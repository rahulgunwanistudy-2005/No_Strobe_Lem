# Build safety

No Strobe-lem is a viewing aid that reduces flashing according to published broadcast guidelines. It is not a medical device and cannot guarantee that content is safe for every person with photosensitive epilepsy.

Generated clips are prefixed `HAZARD_` and written under ignored `synth_out/` with a warning README. Never autoplay them. Contributors who are photosensitive should review numerical traces and truth sidecars only. Demos must use frame strips or warned slideshows at ≤2 fps for unmitigated content. Release apps must not play raw hazardous footage. S1 implements no player or mitigation and must not be used to certify or publish content.
